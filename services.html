Other services

Not ready for ongoing coaching? Start here.

Sport Science Consultation

Physiological testing is only useful if you understand what it's telling you. This service works with athletes preparing for extreme and ultra-endurance events, taking your test results and turning them into a clear picture of what your body is doing — and what to actually do about it.

  • Support for athletes targeting extreme, ultra and multi-day endurance events
  • Physiological testing and personalised performance profiling
  • A proper walkthrough of your results — what each number means for you, in plain English
  • Accurate training zones set from your own data, not generic percentages
  • Clear guidance on which areas to work on, and how to train to shift them
  • Practical fuelling, pacing and recovery recommendations based on what the testing shows
  • Ongoing support to apply the results, plus retesting guidance so you can see whether it's working

One-off Race Plans

A full training plan for one specific goal, with no ongoing commitment.

  • Free initial consultation
  • A complete plan built for your race, distance and timeline
  • Sent as a full schedule of sessions, ready to follow
  • Suitable for any distance, from a first sprint triathlon to an ultra

Performance Tune-Up

A focused 90-minute session for athletes already following their own programme who want to train and race smarter, not just harder. We review what you're currently doing, find what isn't working, and fix it — so you stop guessing and start making the training count.

Tune-Up Add-Ons

Extras you can bolt on to a Tune-Up:

  • 30-day follow-up check-in
  • Race fuelling review
  • Race strategy and pacing review
  • Strength & conditioning advice
